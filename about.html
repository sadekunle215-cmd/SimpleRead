Who We Are

Built on Trust.
Driven by Truth.

DailyDrift is an independent newsroom dedicated to rigorous, fair, and fearless journalism — serving readers who believe that an informed public is the foundation of a healthy society.

Our Mission Is Simple: Tell the Truth, Every Day

Founded in 2024, DailyDrift was built on the belief that quality journalism doesn't have to be locked behind paywalls, diluted by clickbait, or distorted by political agendas. We cover the stories that matter — from the corridors of power to the communities that rarely make headlines.

We are editorially independent. No corporation owns our newsroom. No advertiser dictates our coverage. Our only obligation is to our readers and to the truth.

Every story we publish goes through a rigorous verification process. Our reporters work in the field, our editors push for clarity, and our standards team holds everyone accountable — including us.

What We Stand For

Six principles that guide every story we tell.

01

Independence

We answer to no political party, no corporation, and no advertiser. Our editorial decisions are made by journalists, not boardrooms.

02

Accuracy

Every claim is verified. Every source is evaluated. We correct our mistakes quickly, prominently, and without excuses.

03

Fairness

We represent all sides of complex issues honestly, giving voice to perspectives that challenge our own assumptions.

04

Transparency

We explain how we report, who our sources are where possible, and how our newsroom is funded.

05

Courage

We pursue the stories that powerful people would prefer go untold — and we publish them anyway.

06

Service

We exist to serve our readers and the public interest — not to accumulate traffic, clicks, or influence for their own sake.
